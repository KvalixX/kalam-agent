# 🏺 Kalam AI: Merchant Success Guide

Welcome to **Kalam AI**, the first intelligent agent designed specifically for the Moroccan e-commerce ecosystem. This guide will help you set up your AI and scale your WhatsApp sales to new heights.

---

## 1. Getting Started
### Connecting your Store
Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** to link your Shopify or YouCan store. Kalam will automatically sync your products, descriptions, and stock levels every 30 minutes.

### Training the "Brain"
Go to the **Knowledge Base** page. Here you can upload PDFs (like shipping policies or catalogs) and link URLs. This is where the AI learns your specific brand voice and business rules.

---

## 2. Managing Sales
### Live Chats
The **Live Chats** window is your command center.
- **AI Handled**: The AI is currently chatting with the customer.
- **Needs Human**: The AI has detected a complex request (like a refund or complaint) and is waiting for you to step in.
- **Take Over**: Click this button to pause the AI and chat manually.

### AI Activity Log
Want to know what the AI did while you were sleeping? Check the **Activity Log**. It tracks every upsell, every resolved inquiry, and every new order confirmed by the agent.

---

## 3. Growing your Business
### Segmenting Customers
The **CRM** automatically tags your customers based on their behavior.
- **VIPs**: Customers with 3+ orders.
- **Leads**: People who chatted but haven't bought yet.
- **At Risk**: Previous buyers who haven't returned in 30 days.

### WhatsApp Campaigns
Use the **Campaigns** tool to send bulk messages.
- **Targeting**: Select a segment (e.g., "VIPs in Casa").
- **AI Copywriting**: Use the "AI Re-write" button to turn a simple message into high-converting Darija copy.

---

## 4. Administrative Mastery
### Global Search (⌘+K)
Use the global search palette to jump anywhere instantly. Just press `Cmd + K` and type the name of a customer or a page.

### Global Exports
Need your sales data for accounting? Go to the **Export Center**, select "Sales & Orders," choose your date range, and download a professional CSV or PDF.

---

## 5. Support & Billing
### Managing your Plan
Visit the **Billing** page to download past invoices or upgrade to a higher tier as your order volume grows.

### Getting Help
If you ever get stuck, click on **Support** to chat directly with a Kalam human on WhatsApp or read our detailed technical documentation.

---

**Kalam AI is built to let you focus on your business while we handle the conversation. Happy Selling!** 🚀
